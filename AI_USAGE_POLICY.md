# AI Usage Policy

## Prohibition on AI Training Use

The source code, documentation, and all other contents of this repository
(**esxictl**) are explicitly **excluded** from use in training, fine-tuning,
evaluating, or otherwise developing artificial intelligence (AI) or machine
learning (ML) models of any kind, including but not limited to:

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Code generation models
- Code completion models
- Any other AI/ML systems

This prohibition applies to **all entities** — individuals, organizations,
research institutions, and commercial companies — without exception.

## Permitted Uses

The source code remains freely available under the terms of the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPLv3)](LICENSE) for:

- Personal use
- Educational purposes (not AI/ML training)
- Research (not AI/ML training)
- Commercial use (under the terms of AGPLv3)
- Contributing back to the project under AGPLv3

## GitHub Copilot and Similar Tools

This repository opts out of GitHub Copilot training and any similar
code suggestion or AI training programs. The author has not consented
to the use of this codebase for such purposes.

## Legal Notice

Unauthorized use of this codebase for AI/ML training purposes constitutes
a violation of this policy and may constitute a violation of the AGPLv3
license terms. The author reserves the right to pursue all available legal
remedies for such violations.
